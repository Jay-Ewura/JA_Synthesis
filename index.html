How it works

  1. Farmers list their harvest

    Add the crop, quantity, price, and a photo. Post a surplus alert when the harvest is bigger than expected.

  2. Buyers request what they need

    Restaurants, retailers and households say what they want, how much, and the most they will pay.

  3. Farmers send offers

    Farmers see every open request and offer a price. Buyers compare offers and accept the best one.

  4. Pickup or delivery

    Choose pickup or delivery and follow the order from Pending to Delivered.
